FAQ

Questions, answered.

What people ask before their first stream goes through LOXEN CUT.

How much does it cost?

Try it free with 2 videos, every feature included. Then it's $33.99 a year ($19.99 with the launch code LOXENLAUNCH), or $69.99 once for life. No per-clip fees: it runs on your own computer, and if you use an AI service you pay that service directly for what you use. See plans.

How do I buy it?

Join the LOXEN Discord and open a ticket. Once you've paid, you get an activation code. Enter it in LOXEN CUT (on the sign-in screen, or Settings, Plan) and it's activated. All sales are final: no refunds, so try the free videos first.

Can I use it on more than one computer?

One account works on one computer at a time. Signing in on another computer signs the first one out, so you can move it to a new computer whenever you like.

Which AI does it use?

Your own. Add a key for Gemini, OpenAI, Grok, Claude or OpenRouter, or point it at a model on your own computer with Ollama. With Gemini it watches each shortlisted moment with sound; other services see a few still frames.

Do my videos leave my computer?

No. Videos, clips and the speech-to-text all stay on your computer. Your AI service sees the transcript and short low-resolution previews of the few moments it is choosing between. Clips only go to YouTube, Instagram or TikTok if you post them.

Which videos work best?

Anything with people talking and reacting: live streams, gaming VODs, podcasts, interviews. Links can be from YouTube, Twitch or Kick, or you can use a file.

How long are the clips, and what shape?

Vertical 9:16 by default, or 4:5 and 1:1, at 30 or 60 frames a second. With a facecam, the camera sits on top and the game below; you choose how much of the game shows.

Can I change a clip afterwards?

Yes. Open any clip to trim it, rewrite the hook and title, switch the caption style or layout, or draw the camera box by hand, then make it again.

Can I clip any streamer?

LOXEN CUT is a tool. You are responsible for having the right to clip and post a creator's content; most clipping programs ask you to follow the creator's own rules.
